Brainy is a fun attention and reading speed trainer powered by Schulte tables.

HOW IT WORKS
Schulte tables are a proven method for developing peripheral vision, focus, and information processing speed. Numbers are scattered across a grid — the goal is to find them in order as fast as possible.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Tables from 3×3 to 9×9 — easy to challenging
- Multiple game modes: classic, reverse, letters
- Levels and achievements system — daily motivation
- Character collection — unlock new heroes through progress
- Daily streaks — keep your training consistent
- Detailed stats and progress charts
- Parent dashboard — track training progress

WHY BRAINY
- Science-based: Schulte tables are used in neuropsychology
- Game format: training feels like play
- Short sessions: 2–5 minutes a day is enough
- No ads: nothing distracts from training
- Works offline

Brainy helps improve focus, reading speed, and overall cognitive performance through play.
