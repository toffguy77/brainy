Brainy is a focus trainer built on Schulte tables — it turns three minutes a day into a habit of thinking faster.

A Schulte table is a classic neuropsychology exercise: numbers or letters are scattered across a grid and you find them in order, as fast as you can. It trains concentration, peripheral vision and visual processing speed — the skills behind faster reading, longer focus and fewer distractions.

FOUR MODES
• Classic — numbers in ascending order, from 3×3 to 9×9
• Reverse — from highest to lowest, breaks the familiar pattern
• Letters — the alphabet instead of numbers, in English and Russian
• Gorbov-Schulte — alternate white and red numbers, the ultimate attention-switching workout
